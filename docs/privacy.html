Privacy policy

Updated 17 November 2026. Fresh Music for YouTube 1.12.0.19. Publisher: Necoma. Support: necomasupport@gmail.com. Independent project, not affiliated with YouTube, Google or Opera.

Scope and local storage

Fresh Music controls desktop YouTube and YouTube Music only. Video IDs, canonical URLs, track titles, listening timestamps, Favorites, the blacklist, custom playlist names and order, saved playlist links, last-track checkpoints, appearance and settings are stored in your browser profile. Separate browsers/profiles have separate libraries. No extension account or analytics service is used. Library data is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, or sent to the developer.

Listening and website data

Recording new listens, using visible watch marks and collecting older visible history are independent options. Skip repeats is off on a new installation. Collection reads loaded supported entries and does not promise an entire account history. The packaged Music adapter reads validated player/queue metadata. Bounded normal requests to YouTube may load additional mix entries. Playback state, listening time, volume and explicit controller actions serve playback only; no general keyboard, pointer or unrelated website activity logging is performed.

Collections and checkpoints

Favorites and blacklist membership are exclusive. Blacklisted tracks are skipped in ordinary queues; a dedicated Blacklist playlist plays them only when explicitly enabled and selected. Import JSON, CSV or Google Takeout files is parsed locally. Exported files can contain the selected IDs, titles, links, order and dates; you choose whether to share them. Automatic playlist continuation stores the last track ID and playlist, not a listening-position timestamp in seconds. It applies when selecting a playlist in Fresh Music and can be disabled in settings.

Required and optional permissions

Required storage saves local data. Required debugger temporarily controls only a validated selected HTTPS www.youtube.com or music.youtube.com source for fixed explicit Play/Picture-in-Picture commands and source preparation. The browser permission itself is broad. The implementation checks URL, video ID and power, uses an owned temporary session and releases it afterward. It does not request Network, Cookie or Security data, arbitrary expressions, unrelated site content or screenshots. Browser debugging notices may appear. Optional scripting and HTTP/HTTPS access display the controller on other ordinary pages after opt-in. Unrelated page text, forms, passwords and unrelated tab titles/URLs are not collected. Protected browser pages are unsupported.

Optional Windows helper and video

Optional nativeMessaging connects to the separately installed Fresh Music Desktop Windows x64 helper. Current supported source metadata, loaded playlist/track lists, appearance and explicit commands travel locally for display and control. The full listening-history library is not uploaded. The helper stores its local window/configuration state, has no startup task and needs the browser/source for playback. Original video uses browser-owned native Picture-in-Picture and is not uploaded or re-encoded by the normal action. Legacy explicit frame-sharing paths, if selected, exchange video only locally between authorized extension contexts without STUN/TURN. Browser privacy settings are not weakened. Exclusive fullscreen/protected surfaces are not guaranteed.

Session state and recovery

Browser session storage can hold source IDs, panel ownership, managed queue/recovery state and per-tab audio preferences. Up to 100 recent navigation IDs/queue URLs and audio preferences may exist in supported tab sessionStorage. Temporary audio entries are removed when the tab closes. Managed playback may create an ordinary YouTube tab; no hidden audio engine is used. Browser exit stops playback. Network, media restrictions and browser lifecycle rules can interrupt playback.

Support messages and optional contact email

The feedback form stores your entered email, topic, message, selected browser and extension version as a local draft. It prepares an email in your own mail application to necomasupport@gmail.com; you decide whether to send it and which sending account to use. Entering an email does not authenticate that address. No mailbox is read. History, open tabs, passwords and logs are not attached automatically. If you send a message, its contents and contact address reach the support mailbox for replying; no third-party form delivery service is used.

External services and updates

Normal media/site requests go to YouTube/Google under their policies. User-initiated helper update checks/downloads contact GitHub HTTPS resources. Signed channel metadata verifies the helper installer before applying an update; this is separate from Windows Authenticode and does not eliminate SmartScreen warnings. The extension does not download remote JavaScript for execution. Opening Patreon or Binance Pay voluntarily uses their own services and policies; Fresh Music does not receive your payment details. Hosting and mail providers may process connection or message data normally.

Access, retention and deletion

Use the Lists page to edit/delete/export collections and settings to disable collection, cross-tab access or Windows integration. Local persistent data remains until you remove it, clear the extension storage or remove the extension/browser profile. Clear fields to remove the feedback draft contents. Session state is temporary. Uninstalling the Windows helper removes its native registration but does not erase your browser music library. To request deletion of support correspondence you sent, contact necomasupport@gmail.com. The developer cannot access or remotely erase your local library.
